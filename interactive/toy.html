what am I looking at

Three kinds of node, two kinds of edge:

user → comment → target

A user authored a comment; that comment targets one or more people. The abuse label lives on the comment→target edge, so a comment naming three people has three edges and they can carry different labels. Red edge = is_abusive = YES.

There is deliberately no player→team edge. Team membership is an attribute, not a link — adding it would invent paths between users and then let you “discover” the communities it created.
